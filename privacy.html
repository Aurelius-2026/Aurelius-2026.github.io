Privacy Policy

Obsidian Calendar · Last updated October 23, 2026

Who this covers

Obsidian Calendar is a private plugin for the Obsidian app, built by Jesse Everett for his own use. It has one user, its owner. This policy describes how it handles that user's Google data.

What data it accesses

  • Google Calendar: the owner's calendar events (titles, times, colors, and links), so they can be shown and edited in Obsidian.
  • Gmail: the owner's email messages and labels, so they can be read and sorted in Obsidian (for example archiving, labeling, moving to Trash, or replying).

How the data is used

Data is used only to display and manage the owner's own calendar and email inside Obsidian, at the owner's direction. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or training any model, and it is never sold.

Where the data goes

The plugin connects directly from the owner's own devices to Google's servers. No data is sent to any other server, company, or person. Calendar and email content is held only temporarily on the owner's devices while the plugin is in use. The Google sign-in key is stored in the owner's private Obsidian vault on his own devices.

Sharing

No Google user data is shared with, transferred to, or disclosed to any third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Obsidian Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by signing out in the plugin's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
