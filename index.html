Obsidian Calendar

A personal plugin for the note-taking app Obsidian.

What it is

Obsidian Calendar shows its owner's own Google Calendar in the Obsidian sidebar, so events can be viewed, created, moved, resized, renamed, and deleted without leaving Obsidian. It can also show the owner's own Gmail inbox for reading and sorting mail.

Who it is for

This is a private, single-user tool built by Jesse Everett for personal use. It is not offered to the public, and it does not have any other users.

How it works

The plugin runs only inside Obsidian on the owner's own laptop and phone. It talks directly to Google's servers using the owner's Google sign-in. There is no separate server, database, or company in between.
